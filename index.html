The Story of Diwali 🪔

After 14 years in exile, Shri Ram, Sita Maa and Lakshman returned to Ayodhya.

On the darkest night of Amavasya, the people of Ayodhya lit rows of diyas to welcome them home.

That is why we light diyas even today — one small light that drives away the darkness.

Jai Shri Ram 🙏

The next day begins the Gujarati New Year — Saal Mubarak! 🎉
